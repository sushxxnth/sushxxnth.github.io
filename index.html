Hi, I'm Sushanth. I'm currently a Master's student in Computer Science at New York University Courant. I also work as a research intern at NYU Grossman School of Medicine, where I study dynamic survival prediction using longitudinal electronic health records.

My research interests lie in machine learning for real-world and scientific systems, particularly reliable prediction, physics-informed learning, and models of complex dynamical processes.

I'm driven by the possibility that AI can help us understand the world more deeply and make a meaningful difference in people's lives. My vision is to build AI that learns from data and scientific knowledge, recognizes its own uncertainty, and earns trust through careful evaluation. Across healthcare and energy, I want to develop models that help researchers ask better questions and turn new understanding into useful decisions.

I'm looking for summer research collaborations in machine learning, AI for science, and healthcare. I'd love to work with people who share a curiosity about difficult problems and a commitment to building AI with real-world value. If that resonates with your work, please get in touch.

Previously, I was a visiting research student at TU Delft, working on physics-informed command validation for power grids, and a research intern at IIT Delhi, studying lithium-ion battery degradation. I also worked on wind-turbine control software at Siemens Energy. I received my B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning from UVCE, Bengaluru.
